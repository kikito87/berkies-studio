Step 2

Turn your photos into
ready-to-post listings.

Add up to 8 photos. Each one gets an optimized Etsy title, 13 tags, a full description in your voice, a suggested price, and ready-to-paste Instagram and Pinterest captions — all informed by what's trending right now.

How this works
  1. Add a photo to each slot. It doesn't have to be a print — your dogs, garden, or a behind-the-scenes shot all work.
  2. Pick the post type: 📦 Product (full Etsy listing + captions), 🐾 Personal (warm caption, no sales pitch), or 🏡 Lifestyle (styled shot with a soft shop nod).
  3. Tap Generate All Listings. You'll get copy you can edit, plus a 🎬 Reel recipe for each — a simple plan you can make in CapCut or Instagram.
  4. Copy what you need. Products go to Etsy; captions go into your scheduler (Metricool). Everything saves automatically.

Shop Health

Audit your existing listings
against today's trends.

Export your listings from Etsy as a CSV and drop it here. The tool checks every listing against current search trends and tells you exactly what to update and why — prioritized by impact.

How this works
  1. On a computer, download your listings CSV from Etsy (the guide link is in the box below).
  2. Drop that file here.
  3. Tap Audit My Shop. You'll get a prioritized list of what to fix first and why, based on what's trending now.
How to export from Etsy
  1. On a computer, open Etsy → Shop Manager → Settings → Options
  2. Click the Download Data tab
  3. Under "Currently for Sale Listings," click Download CSV
  4. Drop that file in the box below (note: only active listings export — drafts won't be included)
